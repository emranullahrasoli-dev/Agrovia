About Us

Rooted in Agriculture, Driven by Passion

We grow quality crops in Kabul using sustainable practices—for Afghan families and a healthier tomorrow.

Agrovia is a family-led agriculture business in Kabul, Afghanistan, founded by Emranullah Rasoli and supported by the Rasoli team—Shaban, Nasratullah, Houda, Safa, Atal, and Baryal. We help local farms grow healthier crops, use water wisely, and deliver fresh produce to markets across the region.

Sustainable Farming
Crop rotation, natural fertilizers, and soil care suited to Afghan seasons and valleys.
Fresh Organic Produce
Vegetables and grains grown with care for families and markets in Kabul and beyond.
